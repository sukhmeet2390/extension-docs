How it works

1

Open Teams in Chrome

Go to teams.cloud.microsoft. The floating Privacy Blur widget appears automatically so you can prep before a client call, webinar, or internal review.

2

Choose what stays private

Toggle messages, names, the left chat list, avatars, meeting details, files, and more before you share your screen with coworkers, customers, or a live audience.

3

Hover to reveal privately

Need to read one message yourself? Hover over blurred content locally while everyone else still sees the blur during the live share.

Frequently asked questions

Does Privacy Blur for Teams work with the new Teams web app?
Yes. Privacy Blur for Teams is built for the Microsoft Teams web experience in Chrome, including the new Teams web app at teams.cloud.microsoft.
Does it send Teams messages to a server?
No Teams message content, names, attachments, or meeting chat text are sent to Amrita Labs. Blur logic runs locally in your browser. Anonymous product analytics and billing events are covered in the privacy policy.
Who is this built for?
Presenters, meeting hosts, trainers, consultants, customer success teams, and internal operators use it to keep Teams chats private during demos, webinars, workshops, onboarding, and recordings.
Can I blur the left chat list and meeting details before I start presenting?
Yes. You can independently blur messages, sender names, the left chat list, preview text, avatars, compose box, files and images, and meeting details before you go live.
Does it work in the Teams desktop app?
Privacy Blur works on Teams in Chrome at teams.cloud.microsoft. The desktop app does not support Chrome extensions.
