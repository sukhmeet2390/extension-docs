## What happened to Automizy?

Automizy was an email automation platform with a popular free subject line tester. Then Cognism acquired them. The subject line tool at `automizy.com/subject-line-tester` now returns a 404. Gone. No redirect, no replacement, just... dead page.

If you landed here searching for "Automizy subject line tester" or "Automizy alternative" — yeah, it's not coming back. Cognism absorbed the team and apparently didn't care about keeping the free tools alive.
