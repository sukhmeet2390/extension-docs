2. What This Extension Does

EmailSubjectScore Pro scores email subject lines as you type in Gmail compose, providing spam, clarity, and urgency ratings with AI-powered rewrite suggestions.

4. What Leaves Your Browser

When you request a score or rewrite, the extension sends only what is needed to provide that requested feature:

  • Anonymous device ID (no name, no email)
  • The subject line text you choose to process
  • Limited diagnostic metadata, such as extension version

Subject line text is used only to provide the requested scoring or rewrite feature and is not intentionally retained after processing. We may use service providers to process requests on our behalf under appropriate safeguards.
