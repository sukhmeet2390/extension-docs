EmailSubjectScore Pro vs CoSchedule Subject Line Tester

Free Chrome extension that scores Gmail subject lines live — vs CoSchedule's free web-based tester. Which workflow is better for you?

CoSchedule’s tester is solid — been around forever, people trust it. But it lives on their website. You paste a subject in, get a score, then manually go back to your email. Which is fine if you do that once a week. Not great if you’re sending like 15 cold emails a day and want feedback while you type.

The real difference is workflow

CoSchedule = leave Gmail → go to their site → paste subject → get score → improve manually → go back to Gmail → paste the better version. Five steps minimum.

EmailSubjectScore Pro = type subject in Gmail → see live score → click “Suggest rewrite” → done. You never left your compose window.

Look, if you already live in CoSchedule’s marketing suite for content scheduling and social, their tester is right there and convenient. But if you’re just someone who sends emails from Gmail and wants help writing better subject lines… the tab-switching workflow gets annoying real quick.

Also — CoSchedule asks for your email to unlock the detailed report. EmailSubjectScore Pro doesn’t ask for anything. No signup, no newsletter, nothing.
