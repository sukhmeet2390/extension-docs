⚠️ Automizy is no longer available. This page compares what it used to offer and suggests alternatives.

What happened to Automizy?

Automizy was an email automation platform with a popular free subject line tester. Then Cognism acquired them. The subject line tool at automizy.com/subject-line-tester now returns a 404. Gone. No redirect, no replacement, just… dead page.

If you landed here searching for “Automizy subject line tester” or “Automizy alternative” — yeah, it’s not coming back. Cognism absorbed the team and apparently didn’t care about keeping the free tools alive.

Why EmailSubjectScore Pro is a better replacement anyway

Even when Automizy was alive, it had problems. You’d get one number — “predicted open rate: 32%” — and no explanation. Cool, but… what do you do with that? Is 32% good? Bad? What’s dragging it down?

EmailSubjectScore Pro gives you three separate scores. Spam risk. Clarity. Urgency. You know exactly what’s broken and which part to fix. And if you don’t want to fix it yourself, hit “Suggest rewrite” and the AI does it for you.

Automizy never had rewrites. It was score-only. So honestly, even before it died, it was solving half the problem.

Other Automizy alternatives (if you don’t use Gmail)

If Gmail isn’t your thing and you want a web-based tester, these still work:

  • Sendcheckit — free, no signup, simple score
  • CoSchedule Headline Analyzer — free with email signup, detailed word analysis

But if you’re a Gmail user — just install EmailSubjectScore Pro. It’s free, it’s inside your compose window, and it doesn’t ask for your email address to work.
