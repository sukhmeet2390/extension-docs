ContactMonkey's TouchStone subject line analyzer is dead. Looking for a replacement? EmailSubjectScore Pro is free and works inside Gmail.

What happened to TouchStone?

TouchStone was ContactMonkey’s free subject line analyzer aimed at enterprise internal-comms teams. It lived at touchstone.contactmonkey.com. That domain is completely gone now — DNS doesn’t even resolve. Not a 404, not a redirect. Just… nothing.

ContactMonkey is still around (they do internal email analytics for enterprises), but they apparently killed the free tool entirely. If you’re searching for “TouchStone email subject line” or “ContactMonkey subject line tester” — it’s dead, and it’s not coming back.

Were they even the same tool?

Honestly? Not really. TouchStone was built for a very specific niche — internal comms at mid-to-large companies. The people sending “Q3 Benefits Update” to 2,000 employees. Their ML model was trained on corporate email datasets.

EmailSubjectScore Pro is for external email. Cold outreach. Sales follow-ups. “Please open this because I need you to reply” kind of email. Totally different intent.

But if you’re here because your old workflow included TouchStone and now it’s gone — EmailSubjectScore Pro covers subject line scoring and then some. Plus it works inside Gmail so you don’t need to paste into a separate tool anymore. One less tab.

The thing TouchStone never did

Even when it was alive, TouchStone gave you scores and tips. Useful. But if you got a low score, you still had to figure out the fix yourself.

EmailSubjectScore Pro has a “Suggest rewrite” button. Score is bad? Hit the button. Get a better version. Done. That alone would’ve been worth switching even before TouchStone died.
