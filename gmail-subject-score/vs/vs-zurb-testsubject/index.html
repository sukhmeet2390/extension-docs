EmailSubjectScore Pro vs Zurb TestSubject

Free Chrome extension that scores Gmail subject lines live — vs Zurb TestSubject, the classic mobile preview tool. They solve different problems.

These tools do completely different things. Zurb TestSubject is about preview — how does your subject visually look in someone’s inbox on iPhone Mail, Gmail app, Outlook mobile. Does it get cut off? Does the preheader flow right?

EmailSubjectScore Pro is about quality — is this subject going to hit spam? Is it clear? Does it create urgency? Can the AI write a better version?

Honestly, you could use both. They don’t overlap at all.

Using them together (actually a good combo)

Here’s the flow that makes sense:

  1. Type your subject in Gmail. EmailSubjectScore Pro scores it live. If it’s weak, click “Suggest rewrite” and get a better version.
  2. Once you’re happy with the content, paste it into Zurb TestSubject to check it won’t get truncated on mobile.

Two problems. Two tools. Neither replaces the other.

But if you only pick one…

If your problem is “my emails aren’t getting opened” — that’s a quality problem. EmailSubjectScore Pro.

If your problem is “my subject looks fine but gets cut off on iPhone” — that’s a rendering problem. Zurb TestSubject.

Most people reading this page have the first problem, not the second. Just a guess.
