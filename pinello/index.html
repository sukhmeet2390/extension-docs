Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers.

How does Pinello work?
Pinello runs inside Pinterest in Chrome. It reads the data Pinterest already sends to your browser, adds score badges in the feed, and helps you score draft pins before publishing.
Is Pinello free?
Yes. The free tier includes feed score badges, basic stats, and 10 AI pin scores per month. You only upgrade if you want more scoring volume, rewrites, exports, or advanced tools.
What data does Pinello use?
Pinello uses Pinterest page data already loaded in your browser plus the title and description text you ask it to score. Image analysis stays local, and AI requests are processed ephemerally for scoring and rewrites.
Does Pinello store my Pinterest content?
No. Pin images stay in your browser. Pin text is only sent when you trigger AI scoring or rewrites, and it is not stored as a content database.
Do I need a Pinterest business account?
No. If you can open Pinterest in Chrome, you can use Pinello. No separate Pinello account is required for the free tier.
What is the refund policy?
Paid upgrades are handled through checkout, and refund requests are reviewed case by case. Email hello@amrita-labs.com and we will help.
