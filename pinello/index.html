Frequently asked questions

Clear answers, on the record.

What does the Feed Score on a Pin card mean?
It is a quick quality signal from the visible cues Pinterest exposes on the feed — image ratio, engagement, and any title text on the card. It is not a prediction of clicks or reach.
Why does the Pin Detail Score differ from the Feed Score?
The Pin closeup exposes fuller data — full title, description, board name, save count, dimensions — so Pinello recomputes the score with more signal. Both numbers are honest for the data they had access to.
What is the Pin Doctor?
A pre-publish check inside the Create Pin workflow. It reviews image format, title, description, topics, and how well your Pin matches the board you selected. Structural scoring is local; AI suggestions and Board Match run only when you click the button.
Will Pinello guarantee I get more saves, clicks, or reach?
No. Pinello does not guarantee saves, clicks, reach, or ranking on Pinterest. It helps you prepare stronger drafts — what Pinterest does with them is outside our control.
What does Pro cost?
$8.99 per month or $79 per year, with a 7-day free trial. There is no lifetime plan. Cancel any time through the checkout portal.
What data does Pinello send to servers?
Nothing while you browse. Only when you explicitly click a suggestion or Board Match does the extension send the relevant draft text (title, description, board name) to our Azure backend for a single AI call. Pin images are analysed locally in your browser and never uploaded.
Do you store my Pin content?
Locally, yes — recent scores are cached so the side panel and CSV export work; the score history is cleared on browser restart. Server-side, we cache AI-generated alternatives for 24 hours and Board Match similarity for 30 days so repeated requests do not double-charge you.
Do you offer refunds?
Refund requests are handled case-by-case at Amrita Labs’ discretion. Email hello@amrita-labs.com.

What is Pinello?

Pinello is a Chrome extension that helps Pinterest creators prepare stronger Pins before publishing. It surfaces a Feed Score on Pin cards, a Pin Detail Score inside the closeup, and a Pin Doctor pre-publish check inside Create Pin. Structural scoring runs locally in the browser; AI suggestions and Board Match run only when the creator clicks a button. Pinello never types into Pinterest for you and does not guarantee saves, clicks, reach, or ranking.
