2. What This Extension Does

Pinello helps Pinterest creators prepare stronger Pins before publishing. It surfaces a Feed Score on Pin cards while you browse, a Pin Detail Score inside the Pin closeup, and a Pin Doctor pre-publish check inside the Create Pin workflow. Structural scoring runs locally in your browser. AI suggestions and Board Match run only when you explicitly click the corresponding button.

4. What Leaves Your Browser

While you browse Pinterest and while the Pin Doctor scores your draft locally, no Pin content leaves your browser.

Data is sent to our backend only when you click one of the following actions:

ActionWhat is sentWhere it goes
Suggest a stronger title / description / topicsAnonymous device ID, the draft title / description / topics, the selected board name (for context), extension versionService providers used to provide the requested AI-assisted suggestion
Check board matchAnonymous device ID, the draft Pin text, the selected board name, extension versionService providers used to provide the requested board-match analysis
Anonymous analytics eventsAnonymous device ID, event name, extension version, browser locale. No Pin content.Analytics service providers

Image analysis is local. Pixel data used for local checks is discarded after analysis and is not uploaded.

Pinello never types into Pinterest for you. AI suggestions are copy-only: they land on your clipboard for you to paste.

Information sent for requested features is protected with reasonable safeguards during transmission.

5. Service Records

Limited service records may be retained for a reasonable period to prevent duplicate processing, enforce usage limits, maintain reliability, and support requested features.

  • Requested AI-assisted outputs and board-match results may be retained only as reasonably necessary to operate the feature.
  • Anonymous usage records may be retained to enforce plan limits and prevent abuse.
  • Technical records may be retained for troubleshooting, security, and service improvement.

These records are not sold or used for advertising.

6. What We Do NOT Collect

  • Your Pinterest account credentials, session tokens, or login information
  • Pin images (all image analysis is local and pixel data is discarded)
  • Browsing history or activity outside of pinterest.com and pinimg.com
  • Your name, email, or other personally identifiable information — unless you email us for support
  • Payment card details (handled by the payment provider; we do not see card data)

Outputs are labelled in the interface, are copy-only, and are not guaranteed to improve Pinterest performance.
