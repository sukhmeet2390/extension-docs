What is Amrita Cookie Consent?

Amrita Cookie Consent is a free Shopify app that adds a GDPR- and CCPA-compliant cookie consent banner to your store, with Google Consent Mode v2 built in. It auto-detects visitor region, adapts consent requirements accordingly, and sends consent signals to Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads. No coding required.
